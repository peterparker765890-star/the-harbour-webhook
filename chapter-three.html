THE HARBOUR CHAPTER THREE

THE FINAL GAME
EVERY SECRET HAS A PRICE

Some debts are paid in money. Some are paid in blood. And some follow you until the very end.

Jack and Daniel stood completely still.

They could hardly believe what they were seeing.

The man standing before them was David Harris.

The same David Harris they had believed was gone from their lives.

The same man who had once worked under Michael Brooks.

The same man who had disappeared years ago.

Daniel stared at him with disbelief.

"David..."

David smiled.

But he said nothing.

Jack looked at Daniel, trying to understand what was happening.

Then, suddenly, Daniel remembered.

Everything began to come back.

FLASHBACK A FEW YEARS EARLIER...

Before James Anderson became the leader of the mining business, his elder brother, Michael Brooks, controlled everything.

Michael was a powerful businessman who controlled mining operations, transportation networks, and several illegal dealings.

At that time, a young man named David Harris joined his organization as an ordinary labourer.

Nobody paid much attention to him.

He was just another worker.

But David was different.

He was ambitious.

He wanted more.

Very soon, Michael began noticing him.

David started doing things that other workers were afraid to do.

He helped Michael's men escape from police searches.

He secretly transported illegal materials.

He carried out dangerous assignments.

And when Michael needed someone eliminated, David was willing to do it.

Slowly, David became one of Michael's most trusted men.

Michael was impressed by his loyalty and courage.

Eventually, David was allowed to attend important meetings.

That was where he met Jack Wilson and Daniel Carter.

Michael introduced them to him.

David remained quiet during those meetings, watching everything.

He learned about their businesses.

He learned about their deals.

He learned how money moved between countries.

Most importantly, he learned how much power Michael, Jack, and Daniel possessed.

But inside David's mind, something had already begun to change.

He started asking himself one question.

Why am I still only a labourer?

He had done everything for Michael.

He had risked his life.

He had killed for him.

He had protected his business.

Yet he still stood below the people sitting at the table.

David didn't want to work for a gangster.

He wanted to become one.

He wanted the entire business for himself.

And eventually, he decided to take it.

THE BETRAYAL

One day, Michael went to inspect one of his mining operations.

Daniel had accompanied him that day.

The two entered the underground mining area.

The deeper they went, the darker it became.

David was already there.

He waited patiently in the shadows.

When Michael moved farther into the mine, David quietly pulled out a knife.

He stepped toward Michael.

But before David could attack—

Daniel saw him.

"David!"

David froze.

Michael turned around.

For a moment, nobody spoke.

Michael looked at the knife in David's hand.

His expression changed from confusion to anger.

"You?"

David tried to explain himself.

But Michael understood everything.

"You were going to kill me."

David said nothing.

Daniel stepped between them.

The situation exploded into a fight.

David attacked Daniel.

Daniel fought back.

The darkness of the mine echoed with the sound of their struggle.

David was young and aggressive, but Daniel had more experience.

After a long fight, Daniel finally defeated him.

David fell to the ground.

Daniel could have killed him.

But he didn't.

He looked at David and lowered his weapon.

"You're young," Daniel said.

"Don't destroy your life."

Daniel chose to forgive him.

Michael, however, never trusted David again.

David was thrown out of the organization.

He disappeared.

But he never forgot what happened.

And most importantly...

He never forgot Daniel.

A FACE STOLEN

David spent years planning his revenge.

Then he found a scientist who specialized in creating highly realistic masks.

David wanted something impossible.

A mask that looked exactly like Daniel Carter.

The scientist refused.

David threatened him.

"If you don't make it, your children will pay the price."

Terrified, the scientist finally agreed.

After weeks of work, the mask was completed.

It looked almost identical to Daniel.

David took it.

The scientist and his family were never seen again.

David disappeared once more.

But now he had a new identity.

A new face.

And a new plan.

THE DOUBLE

Whenever Daniel became drunk during important meetings, David would secretly use the mask.

He would appear as Daniel.

He attended meetings.

He met foreign dealers.

He collected money.

He learned more about the business.

Nobody suspected anything.

The real Daniel would remember very little afterward.

David slowly began stealing money from the organization.

But money was never enough.

He still wanted Michael Brooks dead.

He wanted the entire empire.

He wanted revenge.

But before David could complete his plan...

Michael Brooks was killed.

Ryan had killed him.

David lost his chance.

And when David discovered that Ryan was responsible, he decided that Ryan would pay.

That was why David had pulled the trigger.

Ryan had become his final target.

NOW... THE PRESENT

David stood before Jack and Daniel.

Daniel's face was filled with anger.

"You stole from me."

David smiled.

"Everything you had... I could have had."

Daniel stepped forward.

David immediately attacked.

The room erupted into chaos.

Jack joined the fight.

David was stronger than they expected.

He moved quickly and attacked both men.

Daniel tried to reach him, but David pushed him away.

Jack finally managed to strike David directly in the face.

David staggered backward.

Jack grabbed Daniel.

"Run!"

They escaped from the building.

Daniel looked back at David.

"Why didn't you kill him?"

Jack continued running.

"If I killed him, how would you get back the money he stole from you?"

Daniel stopped for a moment.

Jack was right.

David had stolen everything.

But now they had almost nothing left.

Their business was collapsing.

Michael was dead.

James was dead.

Ryan was dead.

Their transport network was gone.

And David had stolen their money.

There was only one place left.

The mining operation.

BACK TO THE MINE

Jack and Daniel returned to the mining area.

Daniel remembered something important.

Years earlier, Michael had made an agreement with him.

Every major transportation operation would be divided equally.

Fifty percent for Michael.

Fifty percent for Daniel.

It had been built on trust.

Daniel believed that the original agreement documents could help him recover what belonged to him.

"If I find that document," Daniel said, "I can claim my share."

Jack nodded.

They began searching.

The mine was almost completely abandoned.

They searched through old offices, storage rooms, and documents.

Then they heard footsteps.

Jack turned.

David Harris stood at the entrance.

He had found them.

David slowly smiled.

"Looking for something?"

Jack stepped forward.

"Stay away from him."

David laughed.

"You're protecting Daniel now?"

Jack attacked.

The two fought through the mining area.

Daniel continued searching for the document.

David and Jack exchanged blow after blow.

Jack managed to push David backward.

But David suddenly pulled out a knife.

Before Jack could react, David stabbed him in the abdomen.

Jack collapsed.

"JACK!"

Daniel turned around.

His face changed instantly.

David moved toward Daniel.

"You should have killed me years ago."

Daniel picked up Jack and tried to protect him.

David attacked again.

Daniel fought back.

David raised his weapon, ready to finish him.

But Jack, despite his injury, suddenly moved between them.

He protected Daniel.

David hesitated.

Daniel looked around desperately.

Then he saw something lying on the ground.

A gun.

Daniel grabbed it.

David turned toward him.

Daniel raised the weapon.

For a moment, the entire mine became silent.

Daniel's eyes filled with anger.

He pulled the trigger.

BANG!

David fell.

The mask dropped beside him.

David Harris was dead.

Jack collapsed.

Daniel immediately rushed to him.

"Jack!"

Jack didn't respond.

Daniel carried him out of the mine.

For the first time in years, Daniel wasn't thinking about money.

He wasn't thinking about business.

He was thinking about his friend.

THE END OF THE JOURNEY

Jack opened his eyes in a hospital.

The room was quiet.

Daniel was sitting beside him.

For a long time, neither of them spoke.

Finally, Daniel looked at Jack.

"We lost everything."

Jack stared at the ceiling.

"Maybe we were never supposed to have it."

Daniel looked down.

They remembered everything.

Michael.

David.

Ryan.

James.

The drugs.

The money.

The violence.

The endless fear.

Their journey had started with business.

But slowly, business had become greed.

Greed had become crime.

And crime had nearly destroyed them.

Jack finally spoke.

"We stepped into the wrong path."

Daniel nodded.

"And it took almost everything from us."

Jack looked at his friend.

"What do we do now?"

Daniel took a deep breath.

"We stop."

Jack understood.

They decided to end their illegal business.

No more drugs.

No more illegal transportation.

No more killing.

No more running.

They would surrender themselves to the police and face the consequences of everything they had done.

It was not an easy decision.

But it was the first honest decision they had made in years.

Before leaving the hospital, Daniel looked at Jack.

"Whatever happens next..."

Jack looked at him.

"We stay together."

Daniel smiled.

"Always."

Their friendship had survived everything.

The money hadn't.

The business hadn't.

Their power hadn't.

But their friendship had.

And sometimes, losing everything is what finally teaches a person what truly matters.

Sometimes the strongest choice is not to continue fighting... but to finally choose a different path.

THE FINAL WARNING

THE MORAL OF THE STORY

A WARNING TO THIS GENERATION.

Listen carefully, youngsters.

The devil doesn't always arrive with horns. Sometimes, he arrives disguised as a friend, offering you one little escape, one little experiment, one little taste of freedom.

And that's how the trap begins.

Drugs don't ask for your entire life on the first day. They take it piece by piece.

First, they take your control.
Then your ambition.
Then your relationships.
Then your future.

And when you finally realise what's happening, the only thing left is regret.

You think you're in control?
You think you can stop whenever you want?
That's exactly what the trap wants you to believe.

The streets are full of people who once had dreams. Some wanted to become doctors, engineers, artists, leaders. Some wanted to make their families proud.

Now their dreams are buried beneath a habit they thought they could control.

DON'T BE THE NEXT NAME ON THAT LIST.

Remember this:

The real devil isn't the person standing against you. It's the destruction you willingly invite into your own life.

Don't let drugs turn your potential into a graveyard of unfinished dreams.

You weren't born to be controlled by a substance.

BE STRONG ENOUGH TO WALK AWAY.

The world doesn't need another victim.

It needs youngsters who know their worth.

YOUR LIFE. YOUR CHOICE.
YOUR FINAL GAME.

DON'T LET DRUGS WIN.
