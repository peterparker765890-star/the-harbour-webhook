CHAPTER ONE

THE BEGINNING

THE HARBOUR

The evening sky above Tuticorin was slowly turning dark as a heavily loaded lorry approached a police checkpoint near the harbour. The vehicle moved at a steady pace, its large container covered with dust from the long journey. As soon as it reached the checkpoint, a police officer raised his hand and ordered the driver to stop. Two officers walked around the vehicle while another approached the driver's window.

“Open the container,” the officer ordered.

The driver stepped down and unlocked the rear doors. The officers opened them and looked inside. Several beds and expensive-looking sofas were neatly arranged inside the container. Nothing appeared suspicious.

“What are you carrying?” the officer asked.

“Furniture, sir,” the driver replied.

The officer looked through the documents and then inspected the furniture once again. Everything seemed legitimate. After a few moments, he closed the container.

“Alright. You can go.”

The driver thanked him, climbed back into the lorry and continued toward the harbour.

The police had no idea that the furniture was nothing more than a disguise.

✦   ✦   ✦
The Harbour

The lorry finally entered the harbour.

As soon as it reached a quiet area away from the main entrance, several workers rushed toward it. They opened the container and began unloading the furniture. One of the workers dragged a sofa to the side and looked around carefully before taking out a sharp tool. He cut through the back of the sofa.

His expression immediately changed.

Hidden inside were tightly packed packets of drugs.

He opened another sofa.

More packets.

The beds contained them too.

Within minutes, the workers had removed the hidden drugs from the furniture. They carried the packets toward a large ship waiting inside the harbour. One by one, the packages disappeared into the ship's containers.

The furniture was only a cover.

The real cargo was drugs.

And this was only one part of a much larger operation.

✦   ✦   ✦
Jack Wilson

A black Rolls-Royce slowly entered the harbour.

The vehicle moved between the containers before stopping near the workers. Its windows were dark, making it difficult to see the person inside.

The window slowly lowered.

A young man sat behind the wheel.

Jack Wilson.

He looked toward the workers and calmly asked, “Is everything going according to plan?”

“Yes, sir,” one of the workers replied. “Everything is going as planned.”

Jack looked toward the ship for a few seconds.

“Good.”

The window went back up.

The Rolls-Royce slowly moved away.

Jack Wilson was not an ordinary businessman. He was one of the men behind the illegal drug trade operating through the harbour. His business had spread far beyond Tuticorin, reaching places thousands of kilometres away.

But Jack had one unusual rule.

He never used the products he sold.

He didn't take drugs.

He didn't drink alcohol.

He didn't smoke.

To Jack, drugs were simply business. He treated the entire operation like a financial transaction. Nothing more.

The ship eventually left the harbour, carrying its illegal cargo toward its destination.

Los Angeles

After travelling across the sea, the ship finally reached Los Angeles.

Workers unloaded the containers and began moving the cargo. The drugs were transferred carefully, while another shipment was being prepared.

Gold.

The operation was larger than it appeared from the outside.

Then a man casually walked out from a nearby swimming pool holding a bottle of alcohol.

He looked completely relaxed.

His name was Daniel Carter.

Daniel was Jack's childhood friend and business partner. While Jack controlled the drug operation, Daniel was deeply involved in the gold business. Their partnership had existed for years, and both men trusted each other.

Daniel opened one of the drug packets, took some of the substance and consumed it without hesitation. He then took another drink from his bottle.

Unlike Jack, Daniel had no rules about what he consumed.

He was an alcoholic, and his addiction was becoming a part of his life.

But beneath his careless appearance was a man who understood the criminal world extremely well.

Jack and Daniel had grown up together.

Now they had become partners in a dangerous business.

“Friendship built the empire.
Money protected it.”

THE HARBOUR
The Wrong Delivery

Back in Tuticorin, a young man named Ryan was waiting for an expensive package.

He had ordered a new laptop online and paid lakhs for it. The order confirmation had promised that the laptop would arrive within a week.

Ryan was excited.

The laptop was important to him, and he had been waiting for the delivery.

A week passed.

Then another day.

Still nothing.

Eventually, the delivery date arrived.

A lorry carrying online products entered the area.

At almost the same time, one of Jack's drug shipments was moving through another route.

Everything was normal until Jack's men suddenly noticed something.

The police were following their vehicle.

One of the men immediately called Jack.

“Sir, the police are behind us.”

Jack was not inside the lorry.

He was somewhere else, but he immediately understood the danger.

“Keep moving,” Jack ordered over the phone. “Don't let them get close.”

The men continued driving.

Jack remained on the phone, thinking quickly.

Then one of his men noticed another lorry travelling nearby.

It was a legitimate delivery vehicle carrying online products.

Jack's voice became calm.

“Listen carefully. Get ahead of them. We're switching the containers.”

His men followed the instructions.

They forced the delivery lorry to stop at a suitable location on the highway.

The driver had no idea what was happening.

Jack's men dragged him out of the vehicle.

The two containers were switched.

The drugs were transferred into the legitimate product container.

The original drug container was then set on fire.

But Jack's men had one more problem.

The delivery driver had seen everything.

They could not leave him alive.

The driver was killed.

His body was left behind as the burning lorry began to disappear into flames.

The police eventually reached the location.

All they saw was a burning lorry.

They believed the vehicle they had been following had been destroyed.

They didn't know that Jack's men had already escaped with the real shipment.

Jack's drugs were safe.

The legitimate delivery vehicle, however, was now carrying something completely different.

Ryan's laptop was inside it.

Two Weeks Later

Ryan waited.

And waited.

Two weeks had passed, but his laptop still hadn't arrived.

He contacted the online company repeatedly.

There was no proper response.

Frustrated, Ryan went to the police station and began asking questions about the delivery.

During his search for information, he discovered something strange.

The delivery lorry had entered the harbour on the exact day his laptop was supposed to arrive.

Ryan looked at the CCTV footage again.

The lorry entered the harbour.

But it never appeared to leave.

Something was wrong.

Ryan wasn't just angry about losing his laptop.

He was a local man from Tuticorin.

This was his home.

He had seen the harbour his entire life, and he knew how important it was to the people around him.

The thought of drugs and illegal activities spreading through his hometown disturbed him.

He didn't want his society to be damaged by criminals.

Ryan decided to investigate.

He would find out what was happening inside that harbour.

The Harbour

Ryan went to the harbour.

The workers immediately noticed him.

“Go away!”

“You can't be here!”

Ryan refused to leave.

He wanted answers.

An argument quickly turned into a fight.

Ryan managed to defend himself, but after the struggle he grabbed one of the workers and demanded the truth.

“What is happening here?”

The worker looked terrified.

For several seconds, he remained silent.

Then he finally spoke.

“This isn't just a harbour.”

Ryan stared at him.

“What do you mean?”

The worker lowered his voice.

“Gold... and drugs.”

Ryan was shocked.

The harbour was being used as a centre for illegal business.

But Ryan didn't run.

Instead, he decided to expose it.

He needed evidence.

The Infiltration

Ryan gathered information from the frightened worker and secretly entered one of the ships being used by the criminal network.

He waited until the workers began moving the containers.

Then he slipped inside one of the drug containers.

He remained hidden while the workers moved it.

Minutes passed.

Nobody noticed him.

Eventually, the container was opened.

Ryan waited for the right moment before quietly climbing out.

A bodyguard suddenly spotted him.

The man shouted.

Ryan attacked before the alarm could spread.

After a fierce struggle, Ryan defeated the bodyguard.

He quickly took the man's uniform and mask.

Now he looked like one of them.

With the disguise, Ryan could move through the area without immediately attracting attention.

He had one goal.

Find the people behind the operation.

Los Angeles

Ryan eventually followed the information he had gathered and travelled to Los Angeles.

He wore the stolen bodyguard uniform and mask and entered Daniel Carter's residence.

Inside the house, Jack and Daniel were discussing their business.

Ryan stayed hidden and listened.

Daniel turned toward Jack.

“Don't worry, bro. All the documents are in my wardrobe.”

Ryan immediately understood.

Those documents could contain everything he needed.

He waited until the right moment.

Then he quietly entered the room and took the documents.

Before anyone could stop him, he ran.

Jack realized what had happened.

“Someone took the documents!”

Daniel immediately followed him.

Ryan reached a motorcycle and sped away.

Daniel jumped into his BMW and started the engine.

Jack took another motorcycle.

The chase began.

The Chase

Ryan drove through the streets at full speed.

Daniel followed closely behind, but the road suddenly became too narrow for his BMW.

He was forced to stop.

Jack continued the pursuit.

He abandoned his motorcycle and climbed onto a nearby building.

From the rooftop, he continued following Ryan.

Ryan ran through the streets.

Jack pulled out his gun.

A gunshot echoed through the area.

The bullet struck Ryan's leg.

Ryan fell.

Jack moved toward him.

But suddenly, a bus crossed between them.

For a moment, Jack couldn't see Ryan.

When the bus passed, Ryan was gone.

Jack looked around.

Nothing.

Ryan had escaped.

The Return

Ryan returned to India carrying the stolen documents.

He handed them to the police.

The documents contained information about another upcoming shipment.

Ryan and one of the police officers studied the details carefully.

They found the date.

They found the location.

They found the shipment information.

A plan was made.

They would wait for the next operation and arrest everyone involved.

The Trap

The day finally arrived.

Ryan and the police waited inside the harbour.

After some time, a truck entered.

They surrounded it.

The container was opened.

Nothing.

No drugs.

No gold.

The police looked confused.

Then Ryan heard something.

A movement from inside the container.

A man slowly appeared.

He was sitting calmly with a shotgun in his hands.

Several armed men stood behind him.

The man's name was Michael Brooks.

Nobody knew why he was there.

Nobody knew what he was planning.

But they were about to find out.

Michael raised the shotgun.

The first shot echoed through the harbour.

Chaos erupted.

The police fought back.

Ryan tried to survive the attack.

During the battle, Michael shot the police officer.

Ryan was now alone.

He attacked Michael.

The fight became brutal.

At the same time, Jack and Daniel arrived.

Daniel was drunk.

He tried to aim at Ryan, but his vision was blurred and his movements were unstable.

Ryan kicked him.

Daniel fell to the ground.

The fight continued.

Ryan eventually managed to overpower Michael.

Michael Brooks was dead.

But Ryan's fight wasn't over.

Jack attacked him.

Ryan managed to shoot Jack.

Jack was injured but survived.

Ryan continued fighting until he finally managed to overpower both Jack and Daniel.

He tied them to a small boat and pushed it toward the open sea.

Ryan watched as the boat slowly disappeared into the distance.

He believed it was over.

Jack Wilson and Daniel Carter were gone.

The Call

Several days passed.

Life slowly returned to normal.

Ryan finally received a chance to live peacefully.

He sat with his new laptop, thinking about everything that had happened.

The harbour.

The drugs.

The gold.

Jack.

Daniel.

He believed the nightmare was finally finished.

Then his phone rang.

Unknown Number.

Ryan stared at the screen.

After a moment, he answered.

“Hello?”

There was silence.

Then a familiar voice.

Jack.

Ryan's face changed.

A video appeared on the screen.

Jack and Daniel were standing in the middle of a burning forest.

Fire surrounded them.

Hundreds of dead men lay scattered across the ground.

Blood covered the earth.

Their faces were stained with blood.

Ryan couldn't believe his eyes.

Jack slowly looked into the camera.

Daniel stood beside him.

Neither man looked afraid.

“We will be back to kill you.”

JACK & DANIEL

The call ended.

Ryan remained frozen.

The screen went black.

Somewhere far away, the fire continued to burn.

And Jack Wilson was still alive.
