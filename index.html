Every harbour has secrets.

Beneath the ships, containers and endless movement of the harbour, a dangerous network operates in the shadows. One ordinary delivery will bring an ordinary man dangerously close to it.

What begins with a missing laptop will uncover something far darker.

CHAPTER ONE

THE BEGINNING

THE HARBOUR

Near Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu, a lorry slowly approached a police checkpoint. It was late, and the road was almost empty. The officers stopped the vehicle and asked the driver to open the container. Inside were several beds and sofas, neatly arranged as if they were ordinary furniture being transported somewhere. The officers checked the shipment, questioned the driver and finally allowed the lorry to continue.

But the furniture was not what it seemed.

✦ ✦ ✦

When the lorry reached the harbour, several labourers immediately surrounded it. They began unloading the furniture. One of them took a sharp tool and cut open the side of a sofa.

Packets were hidden inside.

Drugs.

The labourers quickly removed the packets and carried them towards a waiting ship. Within minutes, the drugs had disappeared into the vessel.

A black Rolls-Royce slowly entered the harbour.

The window lowered.

Jack Wilson looked outside.

"Is everything going good?"

One of the labourers nodded.

"Yes, sir."

Jack simply looked at the ship for a moment before driving away.

Jack Wilson was a businessman.

But drugs were not something he personally used. For him, they were only business.

✦ ✦ ✦

Thousands of kilometres away in Los Angeles, Jack's ships arrived at another harbour.

Workers opened the containers and began moving the cargo. Hidden among the ordinary shipments was gold.

Nearby, Daniel Carter stepped out of a swimming pool holding a glass of alcohol. Unlike Jack, Daniel had completely surrendered himself to addiction. Alcohol and drugs had become part of his everyday life.

Jack and Daniel had known each other since childhood. Their friendship had eventually become a business partnership, and together they had built a dangerous criminal empire.

"Friendship built the empire.
Money protected it."

THE HARBOUR

Back in Tamil Nadu, Ryan was simply waiting for a delivery.

He had ordered an expensive laptop online and paid several lakhs for it. The company promised that it would arrive within a week.

A week passed.

Then another.

The laptop never arrived.

Ryan contacted the company again and again, but nobody gave him a proper answer.

Finally, he went to the police.

The CCTV footage showed something strange.

The delivery lorry had entered the harbour on the exact date the laptop was supposed to arrive.

Ryan decided to investigate.

He did not want drugs and illegal activities spreading through his society. What had started as a missing laptop was becoming something much larger.

✦ ✦ ✦

Around the same time, one of Jack's drug shipments was moving along the road.

Police vehicles were following it.

Jack was not inside the vehicle.

He was somewhere else, giving instructions through his phone.

His men noticed another lorry carrying legitimate products.

Within moments, the two operations crossed paths.

The containers were switched.

The driver of the legitimate lorry saw what had happened.

He could not be allowed to speak.

Jack's men killed him and set the remaining lorry on fire.

When the police arrived, they believed the burning vehicle was the same lorry they had been following.

The drugs had already been moved.

Jack's operation continued.

✦ ✦ ✦

Ryan eventually went to the harbour himself.

The labourers immediately became aggressive when he started asking questions.

An argument turned into a fight.

Frightened, one labourer finally revealed the truth.

Gold.

Drugs.

International transport.

Everything was happening through the harbour.

Ryan gathered whatever evidence he could find.

He decided to go to Los Angeles.

But after Ryan left, Jack arrived.

He learned that one of his labourers had spoken.

Jack pulled out his gun.

The harbour became silent.

✦ ✦ ✦

Ryan secretly entered one of the ships and hid inside a container.

Hours later, the container was opened.

Ryan suddenly attacked the bodyguard standing nearby.

He took the man's uniform and mask.

Nobody would recognise him.

At least, that was the plan.

In Los Angeles, Ryan entered Daniel's home disguised as one of the bodyguards.

Jack and Daniel were talking inside.

Daniel was drunk.

He pointed towards his wardrobe.

"Don't worry bro, every documents are there in my wardrobe."

Ryan waited for the right moment.

Then he stole the documents.

✦ ✦ ✦

Daniel noticed.

He jumped into his BMW and began chasing Ryan.

Jack followed on a motorcycle.

Ryan ran through the streets and climbed towards the rooftops.

Jack followed him.

A gunshot struck Ryan's leg.

Ryan fell.

At that exact moment, a bus crossed between them.

When Jack reached the other side, Ryan had disappeared.

✦ ✦ ✦

Ryan returned to India.

He handed the documents to the police.

Together, Ryan and a police officer planned an operation based on the transport details.

The day came.

A truck arrived at the harbour.

Police surrounded it.

Ryan stood nearby.

The container was opened.

Empty.

Then footsteps echoed across the harbour.

A man walked towards them holding a shotgun.

Michael Brooks.

Behind him were armed bodyguards.

The police tried to react.

Gunfire erupted.

Michael killed the police officer.

Ryan fought back.

Then Jack and Daniel arrived.

Daniel was drunk and could barely aim properly.

Ryan attacked him and knocked him down.

The fight became chaos.

Ryan finally confronted Michael.

One final struggle.

Michael Brooks fell.

Dead.

✦ ✦ ✦

Jack attacked Ryan next.

Ryan shot him.

Jack fell.

But he was still alive.

Ryan tied Jack and Daniel to a boat and pushed it towards the open sea.

Then Ryan returned home.

For the first time in weeks, he could finally use the laptop he had originally ordered.

Everything seemed finished.

Until his phone rang.

Ryan answered.

He heard silence.

Then a familiar voice.

Ryan looked around.

Somewhere far away, surrounded by darkness and fire, Jack and Daniel were standing among hundreds of dead men.

Blood flowed across the ground.

Flames burned behind them.

Jack looked directly towards the camera.

Daniel stood beside him.

"We will be back to kill you."

JACK & DANIEL
