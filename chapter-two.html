A CINEMATIC CRIME THRILLER

THE HARBOUR

CHAPTER TWO

THE RETURN

The sea was silent.

The small boat drifted endlessly across the dark water. Jack Wilson slowly opened his eyes. His body was exhausted after the brutal fight at the harbour. He looked around and realized that he and Daniel Carter were still alive, but they had no idea where they were.

Daniel was lying at the other end of the boat. He slowly opened his eyes and looked at Jack.

"Where are we?"

Jack looked toward the horizon.

"I don't know."

Daniel looked around at the endless sea.

"So... we're dead?"

Jack gave him an irritated look.

"No."

Daniel looked at the sky.

"Then maybe we're in heaven."

Jack ignored him and carefully studied the direction of the waves. Suddenly, he recognized the area.

"This is close to one of our shipping routes."

Daniel sat up.

"Our ships?"

Jack nodded.

"If we're lucky, one of them will pass this way."

They waited.

Hours passed.

But no ship appeared.

Jack slowly began to realize that something was wrong. Their entire transport network had been exposed. Ryan had stopped their operations, and the police were searching for their men and ships.

Then, late at night, a light appeared on the horizon.

A ship.

Jack immediately recognized it.

It was one of their international ships.

The crew spotted the small boat and lowered a ladder. Jack and Daniel climbed aboard.

The labourers stared at them in shock.

"Sir... we thought you were dead."

Jack ignored the comment.

"What happened to the other ships?"

One of the labourers explained that Ryan had exposed their operations and the police had stopped their transports. The remaining crew had been ordered to stay hidden at sea.

Jack looked at Daniel.

Then he gave one order.

"Take this ship to Los Angeles."

The ship changed direction.

They were going back.

But they didn't know that another enemy was already waiting for them.

✦

After reaching Los Angeles, Jack and Daniel left the ship and entered the city.

They drove through the streets in silence.

Their business was collapsing.

Their routes had been exposed.

Their men were hiding.

Everything they had built was beginning to disappear.

Suddenly, Daniel noticed several motorcycles following their car.

"Jack..."

Jack looked behind them.

A biker raised a gun.

BANG!

A bullet struck the car.

Daniel immediately accelerated.

The bikers chased them through the streets.

More gunshots followed.

Jack climbed onto the roof of the car and pulled out his gun.

One biker came close.

Jack fired.

The biker lost control and crashed.

Another biker approached.

Jack fired again.

Then one biker managed to reach the car.

Jack grabbed him and pulled him inside.

He pointed a gun at him.

"Who sent you?"

The biker refused to answer.

Jack tightened his grip.

"Who?"

Finally, the biker spoke.

"James Anderson."

Jack and Daniel looked at each other.

James Anderson.

Michael Brooks's younger brother.

The same Michael Brooks who had been killed at the harbour.

James believed Jack and Daniel were responsible.

And now he wanted revenge.

✦

Jack and Daniel went directly to James Anderson's mansion.

Security guards surrounded them.

James eventually appeared at the top of the stairs.

"You killed my brother."

Jack shook his head.

"We didn't."

James refused to believe them.

Jack then showed him the transport documents proving that Ryan had exposed their operations and caused their entire network to collapse.

James studied the documents.

After a long silence, he finally said:

"Take them to Tuticorin."

James ordered his guards to accompany Jack and Daniel.

"If Ryan killed my brother, we'll finish him."

The vehicles began their journey toward India.

But James had already made another plan.

✦

Before reaching the harbour, the vehicles suddenly turned into a forest.

Jack immediately became suspicious.

"This isn't the way to the harbour."

Nobody answered.

The vehicles stopped.

The guards dragged Jack and Daniel out and tied them to trees.

A phone rang.

One of the guards answered.

James Anderson was on the other side.

Jack shouted:

"James!"

James laughed.

"I never believed you."

Jack stared at him.

"You know we didn't kill Michael."

"I know."

Jack's expression changed.

James continued.

"I don't need either of you anymore. Once you're dead, I'll take over the entire business."

Daniel shouted angrily.

"You bastard!"

James ended the call.

The guards approached with knives.

Daniel struggled against the ropes.

Jack looked at his wristwatch.

He smashed it against the tree.

The glass cracked.

He smashed it again.

A sharp piece of glass broke away.

Jack secretly used it to cut through the rope.

A guard approached him with a knife.

At the last moment, Jack broke free.

He attacked the guard.

Daniel freed himself and joined the fight.

The forest erupted into chaos.

Gunshots echoed through the trees.

Fire spread across the ground.

The guards attacked from every direction.

Jack and Daniel fought back with brutal determination.

One by one, the guards fell.

When the fight ended, Jack and Daniel stood among the bodies.

Jack took out his phone.

He called Ryan.

Ryan answered.

Jack spoke calmly.

"We will be back to kill you."

The call ended.

Ryan stood frozen.

He knew the nightmare had returned.

✦

The next day, Ryan went to the harbour.

He knew Jack and Daniel would come looking for him.

They were already there.

Ryan saw them.

For a moment, nobody moved.

Then Daniel attacked.

The harbour once again became a battlefield.

Ryan fought back with everything he had.

But Jack and Daniel eventually overpowered him.

Daniel grabbed Ryan.

"You destroyed everything."

Ryan shouted back:

"You were destroying my home!"

Daniel attacked him.

Jack stepped forward.

"We're taking you to Los Angeles."

Ryan had no choice.

They took him with them.

✦

In Los Angeles, they brought Ryan directly to James Anderson.

James looked at the three men standing before him.

Jack.

Daniel.

Ryan.

A strange smile appeared on James's face.

"So this is Ryan."

Ryan stared at him.

James walked closer.

"You caused me a lot of trouble."

Then James suddenly raised his gun.

Jack realized what was happening.

"James..."

James smiled.

"I'm going to solve everything."

He pointed the weapon at all three of them.

"I don't need any of you."

A fight immediately broke out.

Jack attacked.

Daniel followed.

Ryan joined them.

The room descended into chaos.

Furniture shattered.

Glass broke.

Gunshots echoed through the building.

James fought desperately.

But Ryan finally reached him.

The two struggled.

Ryan found an opening.

He attacked James.

James fell to the ground.

James Anderson was dead.

Silence returned to the room.

Ryan looked at Jack and Daniel.

"I don't want your business."

He paused.

"Let me live. I won't involve myself in your problems anymore."

Daniel stared at him.

"You deserved to die."

Ryan looked at Daniel.

Before anyone could say another word—

BANG!

A single gunshot echoed through the room.

Ryan's body suddenly dropped to the floor.

Silence.

Jack froze.

Daniel froze.

Neither of them had fired.

Slowly, they turned around to see who had pulled the trigger.

And then—

They were shocked.

A man was standing behind them.

He was wearing a dark mask.

But the face on the mask...

It looked exactly like Daniel.

Jack stared at him.

Daniel stared at himself.

For a moment, neither of them could understand what they were seeing.

"Who... is that?" Daniel whispered.

The mysterious man didn't answer.

He simply stood there.

Then, slowly...

He raised his hand toward his face.

His fingers touched the edge of the mask.

Jack's eyes widened.

Daniel took a step backward.

The man slowly began removing the mask.

Piece by piece...

Until the mask finally came off.

It fell silently to the floor.

Jack and Daniel stared at the man's face.

Their expressions changed instantly.

They knew him.

They had never expected to see him here.

Jack and Daniel looked at each other.

Then back at the man.

And together, in complete shock, they said—

"David Harris?"

David Harris slowly smiled.

The room fell silent.

Ryan was dead.

James Anderson was dead.

And now...

David Harris had arrived.

THE END...
