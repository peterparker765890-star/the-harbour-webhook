THE PAST NEVER STAYS BURIED.

Jack Wilson and Daniel Carter survived the fire. But survival was only the beginning. Across the ocean, old enemies are waiting—and the next move will change everything.

Scene 01 · Open Water

THE LONG WAY BACK

The sea stretched endlessly around the small boat. Jack Wilson and Daniel Carter sat exhausted, their clothes soaked and their bodies battered by the waves. Behind them lay the harbour, the fire, and the secrets that had nearly cost them their lives.

Hours passed before the shape of an international cargo ship appeared through the mist. With the last of their strength, they signalled to the crew and were pulled aboard.

Jack stood at the railing, watching the water disappear beneath the ship. Daniel came to stand beside him.

“Where now?” Daniel asked.

Jack looked toward the horizon.

“Los Angeles. We have unfinished business.”

The ship turned west. Neither man knew what was waiting for them on the other side of the world.

Scene 02 · Los Angeles

THE MESSAGE IN THE STREETS

Los Angeles welcomed them with restless traffic, neon lights, and a city that never seemed to sleep. Jack and Daniel were driving through the city when several bikers suddenly surrounded their car.

Metal struck glass. The street erupted into chaos. Jack fought his way out and caught one of the bikers before he could escape.

Jack grabbed him by the jacket and stared directly into his eyes.

“Who sent you?”

The biker hesitated. Jack tightened his grip.

“James Anderson,” the biker finally said. “Michael Brooks's younger brother.”

For a moment, Jack said nothing. Michael was dead, but his name was still pulling people into the darkness. James had inherited more than his brother's fortune. He had inherited his grudges.

Jack released the biker and turned to Daniel.

“Take me to James.”
Scene 03 · The Mansion

A FRIENDLY FACE

James Anderson's mansion stood behind tall gates, guarded by men who watched every movement. James received Jack and Daniel with a calm smile. The expensive room, polished furniture, and relaxed voice made the meeting seem almost friendly.

He listened as they explained what had happened at the harbour. He nodded at the right moments and offered to help them return to Tuticorin.

“You can trust me,” James said.

Jack studied him. Something about the smile felt wrong, but he had no proof. Daniel gave a slight nod, and they accepted the offer.

As soon as the two men left the room, James's expression changed. He turned to his guards.

James Anderson · Private Order

“Make sure they never reach the harbour.”

His men understood. The journey to Tuticorin was never meant to end at the harbour.

Scene 04 · The Forest Road

THE TRAP CLOSES

The vehicle left the main road. Buildings gave way to trees, and the road grew narrower with every turn. Jack noticed the change first.

“This isn't the way to the airport,” Jack said.

The guards did not answer. The vehicle stopped deep inside the forest. Before Jack and Daniel could react, the guards dragged them out and tied them to separate trees.

Then James appeared, stepping out of the shadows as if he had been waiting for the moment all along.

“You actually believed I was going to help you?”

Jack stared at him. James explained that he had never trusted them. With Jack and Daniel gone, he planned to take control of the business and remove every obstacle in his path.

James walked away, leaving his men behind. Jack twisted against the ropes, searching the ground. Near his hand lay a sharp piece of broken glass from his shattered watch.

He worked the glass against the rope until the fibres began to split. One final pull, and his hands came free. He moved quickly to Daniel and cut him loose.

The guards turned at the sound. The forest erupted into a brutal struggle. Jack and Daniel fought their way through the men, refusing to be buried in a place nobody would ever search.

In the confusion, flames caught the dry brush and began spreading between the trees. When the last guard fell, the two men escaped through the smoke. Behind them, the forest glowed red against the night.

Jack took out his phone and called Ryan.

“We will be back to kill you.”

He ended the call before Ryan could answer.

Scene 05 · Tuticorin Harbour

BACK WHERE IT BEGAN

The journey back was quieter than the first. Jack and Daniel had no interest in explanations, apologies, or deals. They wanted Ryan—and this time, they intended to leave with him.

At Tuticorin harbour, the air carried the smell of salt, fuel, and smoke. Ships stood like dark giants beneath the night sky. Ryan saw them approaching and tried to escape, but Jack cut off his path while Daniel moved in from the other side.

The fight was fast and brutal. Ryan fought desperately, but Jack and Daniel had come prepared. They overpowered him and forced him into their vehicle.

Ryan looked from one man to the other, finally understanding that the past had caught up with him.

“Los Angeles,” Jack said. “You're coming with us.”
Scene 06 · The Final Meeting

NO MORE ALLIES

Back in Los Angeles, James gathered Jack, Daniel, and Ryan in a secluded room. The three men stood facing him, each carrying a different reason to hate him. James looked at them with cold amusement.

“I don't need any of you anymore.”

He raised his gun. For a heartbeat, nobody moved. Then the room exploded into action. Jack lunged for cover, Daniel struck back, and Ryan seized the opening.

In the confusion, Ryan reached James first. A violent struggle followed—and James fell, killed by the man he had tried to control.

Silence swallowed the room. Ryan backed away, breathing hard. The confidence he had carried for so long was gone.

“Please,” Ryan said. “Let me live. I won't get involved again. I swear.”

Jack did not answer. Daniel remained still. Ryan looked from one face to the other, searching for mercy.

Then a gunshot shattered the silence.

Ryan dropped to the floor. Jack and Daniel stared at him—and then at each other. Neither of them had fired.

Scene 07 · The Stranger

THE FACE BEHIND THE MASK

A figure stood in the doorway. Dark clothes. A weapon in hand. A mask covering the face.

Jack slowly turned toward the stranger. Daniel stepped forward, trying to understand what he was seeing. The mask was shaped exactly like Daniel's face—as if someone had taken his identity and turned it into a disguise.

The stranger did not speak. He simply reached up and removed the mask.

THE MASK COMES OFF

The face beneath it stopped both men cold.

Jack's eyes widened. Daniel could barely form the words.

“David Harris?”

The name hung in the air. Every answer they thought they had found suddenly became another question.

One identity revealed. A thousand questions remain.
